# CHMS-F001 decision register

Feature: [CHMS-F001 — Church, Campus and Person Foundation](../features/CHMS-F001/specification.md)
Last updated: 2026-10-27

Status values:
- **APPROVED**: approved by the product owner.
- **PROPOSED**: technical decision, not approved.
- **REVIEW PENDING**: an existing record whose approval has not been verified.
- **DEFERRED**: deliberately left for later.

Approving the business rules and policies below does **not** approve any technical mechanism. Technical decisions stay PROPOSED until the product owner approves their exact ADR text.

## 3. Proposed product-owner decisions awaiting confirmation

These were proposed in review on 2026-10-27. They are **not approved** until the product owner confirms them explicitly.

| ID | Proposed decision | Scope | Status | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPD-01 | Campus names are unique within a church, including archived records. Leading and trailing whitespace is trimmed. Comparison is case-insensitive. Names are normalized to Unicode NFC. Length is 1–120 characters after trimming. Display capitalization is preserved as entered. The exact PostgreSQL implementation remains Proposed (TD-09, ADR-008). | Campus names only. Church names, person display names and name components are not covered. | AWAITING CONFIRMATION | RR-16 |

Confirmation also needs to settle three details:
- whether "characters" means Unicode code points after NFC (the proposed technical reading);
- which characters count as whitespace for trimming;
- whether the same rules apply to church and person names.
