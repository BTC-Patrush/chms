# ADR-002 — Archive and deletion
Status: Accepted, with amendment
Date: 2026-10-27
Decision: Archive-first, domain-specific deletion and controlled erasure; no blanket soft-delete policy.
Amendment on acceptance (2026-10-27): archive-first behavior is accepted. CHMS-F001 implements no erasure and no deletion path (TD-12). Domain-specific deletion and controlled erasure are not implemented in F001; their rules remain open (RR-10).
