## Approved business rules (CHMS-F001)
Approved by the product owner on 2026-10-27. This is the authoritative text.

- **BR-01:** One church per self-hosted installation, supporting multiple campuses.
- **BR-06:** Campus archival is blocked while active affiliations exist.
- **BR-07:** Campus names must be unique within a church using case-insensitive comparison. Archived campus names remain reserved.
- **BR-09:** Church identity is immutable; authorized renaming is permitted and audited.

These approvals cover only the rules above. They do not approve technical mechanisms (see the [CHMS-F001 decision register](../../decisions/CHMS-F001-decision-register.md)) or any other D-02 requirement, and they do not change MVP scope. Feature: [CHMS-F001](../../features/CHMS-F001/specification.md). Deferred D-02 MVP items, such as campus address and timezone, are listed in the [traceability matrix](../traceability-matrix.md#chms-f001-church-campus-and-person-foundation).
